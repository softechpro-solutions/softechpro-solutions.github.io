How does the €1.99 25-Hour Real Software Test work?

For €1.99 today you receive 25 hours of real software access (up to 10 ECU files) plus +3 non-expiring tuning credits valued at €30 (€10/credit) for Stage 1, Pops & Bangs, E85, VMAX or A2L on 1 PC. Renews automatically to Monthly Basic (€75/month) after 25 hours unless cancelled before the test ends.

Are DPF Off, EGR Off, AdBlue/SCR Off and DTC Off included in daily patches?

Yes. Daily patch allowances cover emissions and diagnostic modules (DPF/OPF, EGR, AdBlue/SCR, NOx, Flaps, TVA, DTC Off) with zero extra credit cost. Extra tuning credits (€10 each on Cars, never expire) apply to Stage 1/1+, Pops & Bangs, E85 and DAMOS/A2L.

Why must every uploaded ECU readout be a 100% Virgin Original Stock file?

Automated calibration switches and checksum blocks rely on authentic factory map structures. Always save a full virgin backup before flashing and never upload previously tuned or DPF/DTC-deleted files.
