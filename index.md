# Softechpro Solutions (v2.0.6) — Automated ECU Calibration, Emissions Diagnostics & Checksum Suite

**Softechpro Solutions (v2.0.6)** is a native **Windows 10/11 & macOS** desktop ECU calibration application and **B2B HTTPS REST API** covering **4,965+ 12V Car & Light Van ECUs** (82 brands) and **446+ 24V Heavy-Duty Truck, Bus, Agricultural Tractor & Construction ECUs** (82 brands).

## Core Capabilities
- **Daily Patch Modules (0 Extra Credits):** Automated DPF/FAP/OPF Off, EGR Off, AdBlue/SCR/NOx Off, Swirl Flaps Off, TVA Off & Selective OBD-II DTC Off.
- **Performance & Credit Modules:** Stage 1 / Stage 1+ Remapping, Pops & Bangs, E85 FlexFuel, VMAX, Hardcut, Launch Control & DAMOS/A2L Map Packs.
- **Built-In Checksum Engine:** Automatic block checksum & CVN recalculation on 100% Virgin Original Stock (`.bin` / `.ori`) files.
